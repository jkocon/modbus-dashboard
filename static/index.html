Modbus RTU Dashboard

Scanning, reading, and configuring Modbus RTU devices — COM/RS485 and network

e.g. Elfin EW11 / USR-TCP232 in transparent mode (port is often 502/8899/4196/23)

Two independent methods at once: a verified UDP broadcast for Hi-Flying/Elfin modules (identifies IP, MAC, and model) and a TCP port scan across the whole subnet (finds any converter with one of the given ports open, regardless of brand — the only way to detect devices with a closed protocol, e.g. USR-IOT/VirCOM).
